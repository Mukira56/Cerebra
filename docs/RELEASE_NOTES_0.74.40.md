# Cerebra 0.74.40

- **Talk to Cerebra from your phone, in your own words.** Type to your Cerebra Telegram bot the way you'd talk to a person:
  - "put dark mode on"
  - "update Cerebra to the newest release"
  - "what's waiting for me?"
  - "send 100 pitches a day"
  - "start a task on the Shop site: add a contact page"
  - "how many replies did my pitches get?"
  
  Simple requests are understood straight away. For anything else, Cerebra asks your own writing model, which can look things up in Cerebra first and then answer you.
- **It works through Cerebra's own settings and checks.** Changes from the phone go through the same rules as on the laptop: a daily number above Gmail's limit is refused there too.
  - Changing how Cerebra looks, or a setting, happens at once, and the bot tells you what it did.
  - Anything that sends email, switches sending on, publishes, pays, approves, merges or installs asks you "yes or no?" first, typed or tapped.
- **Update from your phone.** "Update Cerebra" checks for a newer release. When you say yes, it downloads and verifies it, then installs it: Cerebra closes, updates and reopens on its own, and the bot tells you when the install starts.
- **Dark mode and text size follow you.** How Cerebra looks is now kept in Cerebra itself, so a change made from the phone or another window shows on every open window within a few seconds.
- **What stays on the laptop:** connecting accounts, keys, tokens and passwords are never handled over Telegram, and nothing can be deleted from the phone. Only your own Telegram chat is answered.
