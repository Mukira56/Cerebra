# Cerebra 0.74.16

Cerebra now backs up your records every day. Each copy is checked before it counts, and the last 7 days and 4 weeks are kept. You'll find them under Settings > Backups, where you can also back up now, add a second folder such as an external drive, and go back to an earlier date. Going back finishes the next time you open Cerebra, and your current data is kept first.

Backups cover Cerebra's records. Book files and covers aren't included, and on another computer you'd connect your accounts again.
