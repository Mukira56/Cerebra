# Cerebra 0.74.24

New page: **Company pitches** (Earn › Company pitches). Offer your service to companies by email from your own Gmail.

- **Find companies:** one click lists AI, language-data and translation companies. It starts with a built-in list and adds every company Wikidata lists in these fields, about 1,200 in all.
- **Read their websites:** Cerebra reads each company's own website for a public role address (partnerships@, vendors@, hello@ and the like) and for its pages for vendors, partners, linguists and contributors. A person's own address is never used, and nothing is guessed.
- **Send your pitch:** write it once, using {company} for each company's name. Cerebra sends it from your Gmail with your name, postal address and an opt-out line, within a daily number that starts at 10. It sends one follow-up in the same thread.
- **Replies:** a reply stops the follow-up and tells you. "No thanks" or a bounce means that company is never written to again.
- **Application forms:** companies that take applications only through a form are listed with a button to open the page. Fill in the form yourself, then mark it applied.
