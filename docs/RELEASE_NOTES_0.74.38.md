# Cerebra 0.74.38

- **Prepare for the interview.** When an employer you applied to invites you, Apply for jobs prepares notes for you:
  - what the employer does and what the role is for
  - the questions they're likely to ask, with what your CV says for each
  - good questions to ask them
  - anything the posting asks for that your CV doesn't show
  
  The notes use only your CV, the posting, the employer's website and their reply. A figure that doesn't come from those is refused.
- **Automatic emails go out during working hours.** Company pitches and job applications that Cerebra sends on its own now go out Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm on this computer's clock, when people read email. Outside those hours Cerebra keeps researching, drafting and reading replies. Your own Send button always sends at once. Change the hours, or turn this off, on each page.
