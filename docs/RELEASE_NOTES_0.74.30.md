# Cerebra 0.74.30

**A new, easier look**
- **Light and clear by default:** large, plain text without the typewriter font, big buttons and white forms. Prefer the old dark look? One click on **Dark**.
- **Bigger text whenever you want it:** the **A / A+ / A++** buttons in the menu, or Settings › How Cerebra looks. Every page follows your choice.
- **A colour for each kind of work:** blue for Home, green for making money, purple for building software, teal for what happened, and grey for settings. Each page opens with its colour, so you always know where you are.
- **Plain names:** Home, Waiting for my OK, Ask for help, Pitch companies, Find work, Money & invoices, Write books, Sell on Etsy, Connected accounts and more.
- **A simpler menu:** only the everyday pages show at first. **Show all pages** brings back the coding and other tools.
- **Home asks "What would you like to do today?"** and gives a big button for each everyday job, with a count when something waits for your OK.
