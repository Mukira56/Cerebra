# Cerebra 0.74.21

The Books page has a new panel, Learn from what's selling. Paste the top books in a niche from Amazon, one per line, and Cerebra tells you how many really sell, the typical price and what you'd earn per copy, how hard the competition is, and the title words buyers keep seeing. Make my own version then builds your original book to that plan: your own pages, cover and listing, in the size and length that sell.

Only public facts are read, never another book's cover, text or layout. Your AI is told not to imitate those titles, and a title that still comes out too close is flagged before you upload, because KDP removes books for that.
