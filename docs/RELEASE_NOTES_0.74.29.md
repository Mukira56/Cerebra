# Cerebra 0.74.29

**Company pitches: deep research on every company, kept in Cerebra**
- Cerebra studies each company's own website over days and weeks, a few pages at a time and politely: about, products, platform, research, AI, languages, markets, customers, pricing, careers and news. Every page is kept in Cerebra's database, in that company's own records.
- When enough is read, your writing model writes a research summary on each company: what it sells, its AI work, the languages and markets it serves, what it is hiring for and focusing on, and specific facts, each with the page it came from.
- Each pitch paragraph is written from that research and your **market facts** (figures with their sources, which you can edit on the page). It shows the company has been studied, makes the business case in its own terms, and hints at your plan without giving it away.
- **Every figure is checked:** a paragraph with a number that isn't in the company's research or your market facts is never sent.
- Companies about to be pitched are researched first. A company still being researched waits its turn.
- Open any company to see what Cerebra has learned about it, or press **Research them and write it** to do it now.
