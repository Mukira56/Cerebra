# Cerebra 0.74.33

**Apply for jobs: a new page**
- **Finds tech jobs worldwide:** remote, hybrid or on-site, from Remote OK, Remotive, Himalayas, Jobicy, Arbeitnow and Hacker News's monthly "Who is hiring?". You choose the kinds of work, words a job should mention and words to skip.
- **Studies each employer from its own website,** with the same research records Pitch companies uses.
- **Writes one application per job, from your CV.** It opens with what the employer is building, matches the job's main requirements to your real experience, and offers one concrete idea for their business that you'd share in an interview. Nothing about you is claimed that your CV doesn't show, and every figure is checked against your CV, the posting, the research or your market facts.
- **Sends it to the address the job posting gives,** from your Gmail. Jobs that take applications only on their own site get the letter ready to copy and paste.
- **Review ready applications** lets you read them all and send the ticked ones with one click, or you can let it send automatically.
- **Replies:** one follow-up goes in the same thread. An interview or offer gets a reply drafted for you; a no stops there.

**Pitch companies**
- **Any kind of company:** choose software, IT services, fintech, e-commerce, telecoms, internet, games, media, advertising, health, education, banking, consulting, logistics, retail or energy, as well as AI and language technology.
- **A stronger business case in every email,** built from what each company says it wants: the revenue or market your offer could open for it, or the cost, time or risk it could save.
- **No daily number of Cerebra's own.** Gmail's limit is the only one: about 500 a day on a personal Gmail, 2,000 on Google Workspace. When Gmail refuses, sending waits. Sending still pauses if many emails bounce, to protect your Gmail.
- **Market facts now start empty.** Add the figures for your own market, each with its source.
