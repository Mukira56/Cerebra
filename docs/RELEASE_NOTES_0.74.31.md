# Cerebra 0.74.31

**Pitch companies: who made you an offer, and who didn't**
- **Two new buttons:** *Offered me something* and *Not offered*, each with its count. They cover every company you have pitched.
- **Every reply is read in full.** Your writing model decides whether it is an offer: they say yes, want a call, a test or pilot, or ask for your rates, samples or availability. Otherwise it is a no, or no offer yet.
- **Offers get a reply drafted for you.** It thanks them and answers what they asked, without inventing prices, dates or attachments. Read it, change it, and press *Send my reply from Gmail*; it goes in the same email thread. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **A no stops there:** no draft and nothing sent.
- **A company that replies is never pitched again.** This applies whatever it said, to its address and its whole company.
- Replies that arrived while your AI helpers were off are read on the next run, or when you press *Read the replies*.

**A calmer look**
- Text, buttons and page headers are back to a moderate size. The look stays clear and friendly without being oversized.
- Text size and light or dark colours are now only in **Settings › How Cerebra looks**, not in the menu.

**Ready emails, reviewed and sent with one click**
- **Review ready emails** shows every pitch that is written and ready, in full, with each company's own paragraph. They are all ticked; untick any you don't want, then press **Send all selected**. They go from your Gmail within your daily number, and the rest wait for tomorrow.

**Research starts by itself**
- When Cerebra finds a company, it starts studying that company's website straight away, and goes on every 20 minutes: more companies and pages each run than before.
- Each researched company's email is written ahead of time, so the review list fills up by itself. Up to 30 are kept ready.
- A sending problem, such as Gmail needing to be reconnected, no longer holds up research and drafting.
