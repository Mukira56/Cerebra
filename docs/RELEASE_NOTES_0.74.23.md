# Cerebra 0.74.23

When your writing model hits a usage limit and your other subscription can't take over, Prospects now says why: for example, that Claude isn't confirmed as signed in with your subscription, or that it has reached its limit too. That tells you which one to fix.

After a usage limit, automatic outreach now waits 30 minutes before writing again, instead of asking an exhausted model every run. Replies to your emails are still read the whole time.
