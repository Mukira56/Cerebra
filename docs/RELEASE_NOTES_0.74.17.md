# Cerebra 0.74.17

Prospects now looks for the websites of businesses the map lists without one. It tries the likely addresses and keeps a site only when the page shows the business's full name and its phone, street, or city and state. A found business is then read and drafted like any other, and one with no site is marked as a good one to call. Nothing is sent.

Freelance scoring now recognizes more work that isn't yours, such as filming, photography, paid ads and social media marketing. These stay below automatic bidding. Work in a service you chose is never marked down, jobs to build something for those trades, such as a video editor app or a website for a lawyer, are left alone, and anything you add to your own keywords overrides the rule.
