# Cerebra 0.74.39

- **A CV tailored to each job.** Apply for jobs can make a version of your CV for each job, with what the posting asks for first. It uses only the facts in your CV: a skill your CV doesn't name is dropped, and a figure that isn't in your CV is refused.
  - It goes with your application email as a plain PDF that applicant tracking systems can read. You can turn this off on the page.
  - **Tailor my CV for this job** and **Save the PDF** let you look at it first, or upload it to an application form.
- **Test a second subject line.** On Company pitches, add a second subject line and half the companies get it. Cerebra counts replies and offers for each subject line. Once each has 30 pitches, it says which one works better, but only when the difference is clear. Each company always gets the same subject line, so the email you review is the one that goes out.
