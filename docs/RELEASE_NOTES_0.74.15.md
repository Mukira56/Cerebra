# Cerebra 0.74.15

Etsy listings now get a score out of 100 for how their title and tags are built. It checks that the keyword you researched is in the title and near the start, and names single-word tags, tags that add nothing to another tag, and titles that repeat a word. This grades structure, not sales.

Automatic outreach now builds up on a new sender: at most 10 emails a day in the first week, 20 in the second and 30 in the third, then your own daily number. Prospects shows when this is holding you below your number. Emails you send by hand are never held back.

The KDP keyword check now shows empty boxes on one line.
