# Cerebra 0.74.43

**Get set up: setup made simple for first-timers**
- **A new Get set up page** (in the menu, and as a banner on Home until you're done). First, pick what you want Cerebra to do:
  - get a job
  - find freelance clients
  - pitch companies by email
  - build software with AI
  - write and sell books
  - sell on Etsy
  - send invoices and get paid
  - run Cerebra from your phone
- **Only what your goals need, in order.** Cerebra then lists only the connections those goals need, in the order they depend on each other. Each one says why it matters and what to do, with an estimate of the minutes left.
- **Steps check themselves.** Each step turns green by itself once it's done, read from Cerebra's own records with no waiting.
- **One clear next step.** Its button opens the right page and jumps straight to the right panel, highlighted, with a **Back to setup** bar at the top. Optional steps can be skipped and picked up later.
- **A shorter menu.** Under Make money it shows only the pages for your goals. **Show all pages** brings back the rest.
- **Do your first thing.** Once a goal's steps are done, one button gets it going, for example **Find jobs that fit you** or **Check for paid work now**.
- The Gmail panel is now called "Gmail: send and read from your own address", since it sends as well as reads.

**Fixed**
- **The Projects page no longer blinks.** It rebuilt itself every few seconds whenever anything in Cerebra changed, and its at-a-glance table and settings notes reloaded each time. It now changes only when your projects or their tasks do.
